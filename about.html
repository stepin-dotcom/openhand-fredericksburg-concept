About us

Our story

Founded in 2002 as a ministry of New City Fellowship. Incorporated in 2004.

Our mission

We serve people in the greater Fredericksburg area through vocational training, job placement, educational services, and mentoring to help fulfill God's purpose in the lives of individuals, families, and communities.

Our vision

Helping people live with a sense of God-given purpose and hope, better equipped to serve their communities.

Our values

Faith · Community · Dignity · Career

Faith

We believe that Jesus Christ is our only hope in this life and the one to come. The Bible teaches that faith without works is dead. Knowing this, our aim is to share the object of our faith, Jesus, with all persons. In doing so, we offer lasting hope to our community.

2 Timothy 1:9–10 · Hebrews 11:1–3

Community

We believe that God created us to live in unity with fellow Christians and to work for the benefit and good of the Fredericksburg community. Our goal is to build up local churches, organizations, and businesses and help equip them to serve individuals and families.

Jeremiah 29:7 · Galatians 6:10

Dignity

We believe that all persons have worth and inherent value. The Bible teaches that all people are made in the image of God. Our desire is to contribute to the redemption of individuals and institutions, so they are better able to fulfill their God-given purpose.

Psalm 8:4–5 · Psalm 130:7

Career

We believe that work is a gift that God has given to his creation. We aim to restore the view of work as a blessing and ensure others have opportunities to use their abilities to flourish.

Ecclesiastes 5:18 · Colossians 3:23–24

Our history

The Open Hand name

The Open Hand name comes from Deuteronomy 15:11. It symbolizes compassion, generosity, partnership, friendship, service, community, and ready to work. An open hand can give and receive.

Open Hand of Fredericksburg is an outgrowth of a vision for a cross-cultural community-development ministry.

  1. 2002
    Pastor Bob Becker founds Open Hand as a ministry of New City Fellowship.
  2. 2004
    Open Hand of Fredericksburg is incorporated.
  3. Summer Youth Intern Program
    A Summer Youth Intern Program (now called GRAD) begins hiring youth from low-income families to work in summer internships with local companies and nonprofits, placing them with job mentors who teach job-specific skills and work ethics.
  4. Fall 2009
    Open Hand begins providing job readiness and life skills training as a paid consultant to James Monroe High School's STARS GED program.
  5. 2023
    21 interns complete the GRAD Summer Internship Program.

Founder & President

Bob founded Open Hand in 2002 as a ministry of New City Fellowship, a cross-cultural church, and became President in 2017. He was raised in Buffalo, NY, in a low-income, single-parent home.

RJ

Ricky Jones

Board Secretary

Ricky also mentors in the GRAD program. He served as an Intelligence Analyst for the Navy and within the Department of Defense and is a Senior Systems Engineer with Parsons Corporation.

SE

Board Member

On the board since 2021. Scott served as a Marine for 30 years, finishing active service in 2017. His career focused on logistics and leader development and education.
