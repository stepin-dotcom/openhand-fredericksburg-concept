About us

Helping people live with purpose and hope

For more than 20 years, Open Hand has walked alongside Fredericksburg-area youth and families, helping them discover their gifts and build a future.

Our mission

We serve people in the greater Fredericksburg area through vocational training, job placement, educational services, and mentoring to help fulfill God's purpose in the lives of individuals, families, and communities.

Our vision

Helping people live with a sense of God-given purpose and hope, better equipped to serve their communities.

Our story

Why "Open Hand"?

The name comes from Deuteronomy 15:11. An open hand symbolizes compassion, generosity, partnership, friendship, service, community and being ready to work. An open hand can give and receive.

Open Hand grew out of a vision for a cross-cultural community-development ministry: churches and communities that aren't divided along racial and class lines, where youth and adults of every background get the tools they need to thrive in life and work.

  1. 2002
    Pastor Bob Becker founds Open Hand as a ministry of New City Fellowship, a cross-cultural church in Fredericksburg.
  2. 2004
    Open Hand of Fredericksburg incorporates as its own nonprofit.
  3. Early years
    A Summer Youth Intern Program, now called GRAD, begins hiring youth from low-income families and pairing them with job mentors.
  4. 2009
    Open Hand begins teaching job-readiness and life skills to James Monroe High School's STARS GED program.
  5. 2022
    Open Hand celebrates 20 years of serving Fredericksburg youth.
  6. 2023
    21 interns complete GRAD with 12 mentors and more than a dozen local host employers.
