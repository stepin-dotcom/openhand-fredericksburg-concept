Virginia tax credit
65%

Neighborhood Assistance Program (NAP)

Open Hand is a NAP-approved organization. Through Virginia's NAP, qualifying businesses, trusts and individuals who give to approved organizations serving low-income residents may receive a state tax credit equal to 65% of their donation, on top of the usual federal charitable deduction.

Credits are limited and must be requested through Open Hand. Call (540) 834-4455 to ask about current availability, and talk with your tax advisor about your situation.
