GRAD Summer Internship Program

GRAD is a summer internship program whose purpose is to propel under resourced youth into the workforce while providing them with the resources that inspire confidence and foster skills that will secure sustainable lifetime employment.

GRAD offers paid summer employment, mentoring, workshops, job preparation, life skills, character formation, and leadership development. The program features education classes in job readiness, character formation, life skills, career counseling and financial literacy, all to add to the student's abilities to prepare for college or other postsecondary education and to be able to have sustainable employment in the future.

  • Five days of training workshops on life and job readiness skills
  • Each intern is paired with a mentor
  • A six-week paid internship with a local business partner
  • At least six hours of volunteering with a non-profit of the intern's choice

2023 interns attended high schools within Planning District 16, with the majority from Massaponax High School (Spotsylvania Co.), Colonial Forge High School (Stafford Co.) and James Monroe (Fredericksburg City), along with interns from Caroline High, Gladys Oberle Academy, homeschool, other Spotsylvania and Stafford high schools, and college students from Germanna Community College and Richard Bland College.

PEARLS

PEARLS is a partnership with Planning District 16 local high schools' alternative education programs. The PEARLS program teaches life skills building, work readiness, and career/college exploration. Together they serve students who have been suspended or removed from regular high school classes.

Providing life skills to students seeking a GED for various reasons in a non-traditional class setting.

Since Fall 2009, Open Hand has provided job readiness and life skills training as a paid consultant to James Monroe High School's STARS GED program, which meets twice a week during the school year and incorporates academic enrichment using practical real-world experiences.
