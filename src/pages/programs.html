GRAD

Gaining Respect and Determination for My Life's Purpose

Ages 14–19 · Summer

Paid summer internships with a mentor

GRAD is our signature program: paid summer employment combined with mentoring, workshops, job preparation, life skills, character formation and leadership development. Interns come from high schools across Planning District 16, including Fredericksburg City, Spotsylvania, Stafford and Caroline.

  • A week of workshops: job readiness, financial literacy, career counseling, character and life skills
  • A six-week paid internship with a local employer
  • A one-on-one mentor who meets with you every week
  • At least six hours of volunteer service with a nonprofit you choose
  • A recognition ceremony to celebrate your accomplishments

PEARLS

Life skills for GED students

School year

Life skills in a non-traditional classroom

PEARLS provides life-skills training to students pursuing a GED in a non-traditional class setting. Since 2009, Open Hand has also provided job-readiness and life-skills training to James Monroe High School's STARS GED program, which pairs academic enrichment with practical, real-world experiences.

Questions from parents

Is the internship really paid?

Yes. GRAD interns are paid for their six-week internship with a local employer.

How much time does it take?

Interns attend one week of workshops, then work their internship for six weeks over the summer and meet weekly with their mentor.

Who are the mentors?

Caring adults from the community. Every mentor completes an application and interview, passes a background check and attends mentor training.

Do you need to be part of a church to join?

Open Hand is a faith-based organization that welcomes youth from all backgrounds.

Where do interns work?

Past host employers include the Ron Rosner YMCA, Fredericksburg Regional Food Bank, Habitat for Humanity, Lloyd Moss Free Clinic, City of Fredericksburg Parks & Recreation, Fredericksburg Circuit Court and local small businesses.
