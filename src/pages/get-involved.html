Get involved

Join the support team

Every intern's summer is made possible by mentors, employers, volunteers, prayer partners and donors across Fredericksburg.

Become a mentor

Walk with one young person through their summer internship. You'll be the encourager, the problem-solver and the trusted adult in their corner.

  • Meet with your mentee once a week during the internship, in person or online
  • Complete an application and interview with the program director
  • Pass a background check
  • Attend mentor training and at least one day of workshop week

Host an intern

Give a motivated young person their first real work experience. Interns arrive having completed a week of job-readiness training, with a mentor supporting them all summer.

Businesses, nonprofits and public offices have all hosted GRAD interns, and several interns were hired to keep working after the summer.

Volunteer your skills

Are you gifted in any of these areas? We need you:

  • Administrative: donor management, office support
  • Technical: web design, media, data analytics
  • Awareness: social media, communications
  • General: events, transportation, volunteer coordination
  • Leadership: mentoring, board or advisory board

Become a prayer partner

Prayer is foundational to all we do. This is missional work, and we'd love you to join us in praying for our interns, mentors, families and partners.

Sponsor a youth
