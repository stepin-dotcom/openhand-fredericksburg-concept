Become a mentor

  • The minimum commitment is one meeting with your mentee per week for the duration of the Summer Internship. Meetings can be in person or online (Zoom, FaceTime).
  • An application and interview with the Programs Director is required.
  • Mentors must pass a background check.
  • Mentors attend the Mentor Training and at least one day of the Workshop Week.

Become an employer partner

Host GRAD interns at your business or organization. Recent employer partners include Stafford Junction, North Stafford Public School (Stafford County), Jus Pop'n, Fredericksburg Christian Health Center, Downtown Greens, Fredericksburg Area Museum, Fredericksburg SPCA and Capitalize Marketing & Consulting.

Become a volunteer

Are you gifted in any of the following areas? We need you!

  • Administrative: donor management, general office support
  • Technical: web design, media generation, data analytics
  • Awareness: social media support, communications management
  • General: events support, transportation, volunteer organization
  • Leadership: mentoring, board member, advisory board
